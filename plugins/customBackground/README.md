# Custom Background

Sets a background image (upload or https link) or plain color behind Discord. Discord's panels turn see-through so the background shows.

Open **Vencord → Themes → Background**. The installer adds that tab to Vencord's Themes page; on builds without it, a **Background** page appears in the settings sidebar instead.

Controls: fit (fill, fit, stretch, center, tile), base color, panel tint and darkness, image brightness and blur, and quick presets. Uploaded images are scaled to 2560 px (GIFs stay animated) and stored on this device; CloudSync copies them to your other devices.
